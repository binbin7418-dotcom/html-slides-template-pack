ANNUAL REVIEW / KEY MOVES

Three things that worked

No new slogans. Just three moves we repeated until they became routine.

01
Risk flagged 90 days earlier
Usage, ticket sentiment and contract dates now feed one health score; accounts under 60 land on a red list automatically. We intervened with 47 accounts this year — 39 renewed.
02
First response in 23 minutes
We killed the three-tier hand-off: whoever picks up a ticket owns it end to end. Median first response fell from 4.1 hours to 23 minutes, with a night rota covering the gaps.
03
Reviews with three numbers
Every QBR now shows exactly three things: depth of use, value delivered, open problems. If it doesn't fit, we cut slides. Decision-maker attendance rose from 31% to 58%.

ANNUAL REVIEW / HONEST GAPS

Where we still fall short

Onboarding takes too long

New accounts reach first value in 26 days on average; our best quartile does it in 11. The gap is not engineering — it is scheduling. Data imports wait on customer IT windows we only chase once a week.

From January, onboarding gets a named owner per account and a twice-weekly chase rhythm.

TIME TO FIRST VALUE
26 days · target 14

Small accounts feel the silence

Accounts under 50 seats hear from us mostly when something breaks. Their renewal rate is 11 points below the book average, and exit interviews keep naming the same word: "quiet."

A lightweight monthly digest and a shared office-hours slot start next quarter — no new headcount, just cadence.
